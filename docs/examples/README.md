# Example vaults

Two made-up vaults, kept with [the Daybook Method](../method.md) through the same week of November 2026. All names are made up.

- [work](work/) is one person's work vault: a project called Apollo for a customer called Acme, two colleagues, Jane Doe and John Roe, a course, and coaching notes in a private folder, `team/`. It is shown as it is on Monday 2026-11-16 at 08:30, before the brief and the close.
- [family](family/) is a vault shared by two parents, Alex and Sam, who each keep their own day pages: their home as an area, with the bathroom renovation and the shopping list part of it, a summer trip, a school form, a parents' evening, Mia's weekly football, routines, and the plumber's quote as a file. It is shown on Sunday 2026-11-15 in the evening, before the family's weekly close.
